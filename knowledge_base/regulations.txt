### Financial Regulations Knowledge Base (FKB)
# Version: 2.4 | Status: Authorised & Active | Compliance Domain: Global Financial Services

- [KB-REG-GDPR-25] GDPR Article 25 (Data Protection by Design & Default) | Jurisdiction: EU/UK | Effective: 2018-05-25 | Version: 2.0 | Applicability: Any system processing EU/UK resident personal data. Requires data minimisation, pseudonymisation, encryption at rest and in transit, and explicit user consent mechanisms.
- [KB-REG-GDPR-33] GDPR Article 33 (Data Breach Notification) | Jurisdiction: EU/UK | Effective: 2018-05-25 | Version: 2.0 | Applicability: Personal data breaches. Mandatory reporting to supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, with automated incident audit logging.
- [KB-REG-PCIDSS-03] PCI-DSS Requirement 3 (Protect Stored Cardholder Data) | Jurisdiction: Global | Effective: 2024-03-31 | Version: 4.0 | Applicability: Payment gateways, cardholder data environments (CDE). Encryption of Primary Account Numbers (PAN) using AES-256 or strong cryptography, masking PAN on display (maximum first 6 and last 4 digits), and restriction of key access.
- [KB-REG-PCIDSS-08] PCI-DSS Requirement 8 (Identify and Authenticate Access) | Jurisdiction: Global | Effective: 2024-03-31 | Version: 4.0 | Applicability: All systems accessing financial networks and payment environments. Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for administrative access and all remote network access to CDE.
- [KB-REG-PCIDSS-10] PCI-DSS Requirement 10 (Log and Monitor All Access) | Jurisdiction: Global | Effective: 2024-03-31 | Version: 4.0 | Applicability: Card payment processing. Immutable audit logs for all system component access, synchronized NTP time, and 1-year log retention with 90 days immediately available.
- [KB-REG-BASEL-01] Basel III/IV Capital Adequacy & Risk Measurement | Jurisdiction: Global/BCBS | Effective: 2023-01-01 | Version: 3.5 | Applicability: Deposit-taking and lending institutions. Tier 1 capital ratios, liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), net stable funding ratio (NSFR), automated real-time credit risk weight calculation, and stress-testing pipeline requirements.
- [KB-REG-AML-01] AML/BSA (Anti-Money Laundering & Bank Secrecy Act) | Jurisdiction: US/Global | Effective: 2021-01-01 | Version: 4.2 | Applicability: All banking, remittance, digital wallets, and loan systems. Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) generation, Currency Transaction Report (CTR) for transactions exceeding $10,000, Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) screening, and OFAC sanctions list screening.
- [KB-REG-KYC-01] Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & KYC Rule | Jurisdiction: Global/FinCEN | Effective: 2020-06-01 | Version: 3.0 | Applicability: Customer onboarding and account opening. Customer identity verification via biometric or government ID document validation, beneficial ownership threshold verification (>= 25%), and ongoing risk rating updates.
- [KB-REG-SOX-404] Sarbanes-Oxley Act Section 404 (Internal Controls Reporting) | Jurisdiction: US SEC | Effective: 2004-11-15 | Version: 2.1 | Applicability: Financial reporting, accounting, and general ledger integrations. Segregation of duties (SoD), automated controls testing, change management audit trails, and dual sign-off for financial adjustments.
- [KB-REG-PSD2-SCA] PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication & Open Banking | Jurisdiction: EU/UK | Effective: 2019-09-14 | Version: 2.0 | Applicability: Digital banking, mobile apps, Open Banking APIs. Dynamic linking of transaction amount and payee, multi-factor authentication (possession, knowledge, inherence), and secure dedicated APIs with mutual TLS (mTLS).
- [KB-REG-ISO-27001] ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Controls | Jurisdiction: International | Effective: 2022-10-25 | Version: 2022 | Applicability: Information security management. Threat modeling, secure software development lifecycle, cryptographic key management, role-based access control, vulnerability scanning, and disaster recovery.
- [KB-REG-GLBA-01] Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act Safeguards Rule | Jurisdiction: US FTC | Effective: 2023-06-09 | Version: 2.0 | Applicability: Non-bank financial institutions and fintechs. Encryption of non-public personal information (NPI), continuous vulnerability monitoring, and annual third-party risk assessment.
