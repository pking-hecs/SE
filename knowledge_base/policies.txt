### Organisational Policies & Controls Knowledge Base
# Version: 1.8 | Status: Approved by Board Risk Committee | Scope: Enterprise Financial Platform

- [KB-POL-AUTH-01] Enterprise Authentication & Access Control Policy | Scope: All Financial Channels | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Requires OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and OpenID Connect for customer channels. FIDO2/WebAuthn or hardware token for privileged and back-office staff. Session timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity for end-users, 5 minutes for administrative operators.
- [KB-POL-TXN-02] Transaction Authorization Thresholds & Limits | Scope: Payments & Transfers | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Transactions under $1,000 processed via single-factor biometric or PIN. Transactions between $1,000 and $10,000 require out-of-band step-up SMS OTP or push notification. Transactions exceeding $10,000 require dual-authorization (four-eyes principle) and compliance check against sanctions list.
- [KB-POL-CRYPT-03] Data Protection and Cryptography Policy | Scope: Core & Cloud Infrastructure | Effective: 2024-01-01 | All sensitive data at rest (PII, account numbers, card numbers) must be encrypted using AES-256-GCM. All data in transit must use TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy. Cryptographic keys rotated annually via Hardware Security Module (HSM).
- [KB-POL-AUDIT-04] Comprehensive Audit Logging & Traceability | Scope: All Applications | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Every financial transaction, balance query, configuration change, and human approval must produce an immutable audit record containing Timestamp (UTC/NTP), User ID/Service ID, Source IP, Action Performed, and Object Identifier. Logs must be written to append-only WORM storage and retained for minimum 7 years.
- [KB-POL-RESIL-05] Availability, Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity | Scope: Production Tier-1 Services | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Production availability SLA of 99.99% for payment gateway and digital banking. Maximum Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of 15 minutes, Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 0 seconds (zero data loss via synchronous replication across availability zones).
- [KB-POL-SDLC-06] Secure Development & Release Governance Policy | Scope: Engineering & Operations | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Static Application Security Testing (SAST) and Dependency Vulnerability Scanning must be automated in CI/CD pipeline with 0 critical or high vulnerabilities allowed. Dynamic Application Security Testing (DAST) and external penetration testing mandatory prior to production release. No code deployed to production without independent peer review and human sign-off from Release Manager and Security Officer.
- [KB-POL-PRIV-07] Customer Data Privacy & Retention Policy | Scope: Customer Data Stores | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Customer PII retained only for active account duration plus statutory limitation period (5 years post-account closure). Right-to-erasure workflows must purge operational data while preserving transaction hashes required for regulatory audit.
