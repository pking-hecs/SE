# System Scope Specification
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-25

## 1. Supported Financial Services & Projects
The system is designed to support the following domains:
- **Digital & Mobile Banking:** User account management, fund transfers, mobile app features.
- **Loan Origination & Credit Assessment:** Application workflows, credit scoring, underwriting.
- **Payment Processing:** Real-time gross settlement (RTGS), ACH, cross-border payments, payment gateways.
- **Fraud Detection:** Anti-money laundering (AML), transaction monitoring, anomaly detection.
- **Insurance & Investment Platforms:** Policy management, portfolio tracking, trade execution.
- **Regulatory Reporting:** Basel III/IV, MiFID II, Dodd-Frank, local central bank reporting.
- **Customer Onboarding & KYC:** Digital identity verification, risk profiling, document OCR.
- **Financial Data Analytics:** Business intelligence, predictive forecasting, risk dashboards.

## 2. Expected System Outputs
The system shall generate the following structured artifacts:
- **Software Requirements Specification (SRS):** Comprehensive technical document.
- **User Stories:** Agile-formatted stories with "As a... I want... So that..."
- **Use Cases:** Detailed interaction diagrams and descriptions.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Testable conditions for requirement fulfillment.
- **Compliance Mappings:** Traceability from requirements to specific regulatory articles.
- **Risk Register:** Identified business, technical, and compliance risks.
- **Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM):** Linkage from source $\rightarrow$ requirement $\rightarrow$ design $\rightarrow$ test.
- **SDLC Model Recommendation:** Justified selection of the development methodology.
- **Project-Specific SDLC Workflow:** Customized phases, roles, and validation gates.
