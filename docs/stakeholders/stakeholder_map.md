# Stakeholder Analysis & Interaction Templates
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-25

## 1. Identified Stakeholders
| Role | Primary Requirement Category | Key Interest |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Customers / End Users** | Usability, Functional | Ease of use, feature utility |
| **Business Analysts** | Business, Functional | Process efficiency, ROI, market fit |
| **Product Owners** | Functional, Strategic | Product roadmap, prioritization |
| **Software Architects** | Technical, Integration | Scalability, maintainability, performance |
| **Info-Security Teams** | Security, Privacy | Data protection, threat mitigation |
| **Compliance & Legal** | Regulatory, Privacy | Legal adherence, auditability |
| **Risk Management** | Risk, Operational | Risk appetite, control effectiveness |
| **Operations Personnel** | Operational, Maintenance | Deployability, supportability |
| **Auditors / Regulators** | Audit, Regulatory | Transparency, evidence, reporting |

## 2. Role-Specific Interview Templates (High-Level)

### A. Business/Product Owner Template
- What are the primary business objectives for this feature?
- Who are the primary personas interacting with this system?
- Describe the "happy path" workflow.
- What are the critical business rules and constraints?
- What defines success for this project?

### B. Security & Compliance Template
- Which specific regulations apply to this project (e.g., GDPR, PCI-DSS, SOX)?
- What are the data classification levels for the handled data?
- What are the mandatory security controls for this transaction type?
- What audit logs are required by law for these actions?
- Are there specific data residency or privacy constraints?

### C. Technical Architecture Template
- What are the legacy systems this must integrate with?
- What are the expected transaction volumes (TPS)?
- What is the required system availability (e.g., 99.99%)?
- Are there existing API specifications or database schemas to follow?
- What are the primary technical constraints or dependencies?
